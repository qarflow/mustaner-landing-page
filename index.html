Course Overview

Strategy That Survives Contact With Execution

One programme. Four phases. Seven outcomes you can take back to the business.

  • 40Hours in the room
  • 10Live sessions
  • 4Phases you finish

The problem

Growing companies in Egypt rarely lack ambition. They lack a strategy that holds: decisions made on instinct, a plan written in January that no one recognises by March, and teams that are busy without moving in the same direction.

What the programme does

40 hours, 10 sessions, in one room in New Cairo. You diagnose your market, choose a strategy you can defend, build the execution layer underneath it, then amplify the same cycle with applied AI.

How the programme works

Four phases, in the order real decisions happen

You bring a business into the room on Sep 28 and work it through every phase. Each one ends with something in your hands, not in your notes.

Diagnose Sessions 1-2

Market Research and Diagnosis

You leave holding a written read of your market and your position in it — PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces and a competitor map applied to your own company.

Decide Sessions 3-4

Strategy Selection

You leave holding an honest inventory of what your company is genuinely good at, a chosen growth strategy, and a competitive advantage you can state in one sentence.

Execute Sessions 5-6

Strategy Implementation

You leave holding that strategy broken into OKRs, owners and resource decisions, with the KPIs and Balanced Scorecard that tell you whether it is working.

Amplify Sessions 7-10

Strategy Using AI

You leave holding a tested AI workflow for every stage of the cycle — research, analysis, scenario planning and performance monitoring — built during four dedicated sessions.

Outcomes

What you leave with

Seven results the programme is built around — what you will be able to do differently once the ten sessions end.

The Full Strategic Cycle

You work a complete cycle on your own company — diagnosis, selection, execution, control — instead of handling one fragment of strategy at a time.

Globally Recognized Toolkits

You apply PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces, SWOT/TOWS, Value Chain, BCG and Ansoff the way operating companies actually use them, not as textbook diagrams.

Highly Practical and Interactive

You spend the 40 hours in exercises, business cases and live discussion, so each framework leaves the room already applied to a decision you own.

A Shift to Strategic Mindset

You read a market before you react to it, and separate the few decisions that shape the next three years from the ones that fill next week.

Bridge Between Theory and Execution

You convert an agreed strategy into objectives, owners, resources and KPIs that survive contact with the operating calendar.

From Operational to Strategic Leadership

You move from managing the work in front of you to setting the direction others work toward, with the language to defend that direction to a board or a partner.

4 AI sessions

Integrated AI for Business Impact

You use AI across four full sessions to run research, build analyses, test scenarios and monitor performance in hours rather than weeks.

Questions

What senior people ask before they book

If your question is not here, send it to +20 109 271 8547 and you will get a direct answer.

What language is the programme delivered in?

The frameworks, models and materials carry their standard English names — PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces, SWOT/TOWS, Value Chain, BCG, Ansoff, OKRs, KPIs, Balanced Scorecard — as they are used in practice. For confirmation on the language used in the room itself, message us on WhatsApp at +20 109 271 8547 before you book.

Do I need a formal strategy background?

No. Session 1 starts at the line between strategic and operational planning, then builds the pillars — vision, mission, values, objectives — before any tool is introduced. What the programme assumes is decision-making responsibility, not prior strategy training: CEOs, founders, business owners, senior managers and heads of departments accountable for growth or execution.

What if I miss a session?

The 10 dates are fixed and published: Sep 28, Sep 30, Oct 5, Oct 7, Oct 12, Oct 14, Oct 19, Oct 21, Oct 26 and Oct 28, every Monday and Wednesday from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM. The sessions build in sequence across 4 phases, so missing one costs you. No make-up policy is published — if you already know a date is difficult, raise it on WhatsApp before you pay rather than after.

Is this theory or applied work?

Applied. Each framework is worked through on real case studies, exercises and business simulations drawn from live client projects, which is how the programme bridges theory and execution. You leave able to run the tools, not describe them.

Can I book several seats for my team?

Mustaner runs corporate training and company initiatives alongside the open programme, so a team booking is straightforward. Terms for multiple seats are agreed case by case — send the number of people and their roles to +20 109 271 8547 and the team will come back to you.

Can I pay in instalments?

Three payment routes are confirmed: Fawaterk card payment, bank transfer to Arab African International Bank, and Vodafone Cash. Instalment terms are not published — if you need to split the fee, ask on WhatsApp at +20 109 271 8547 before you book and you will get a straight answer.

How is the AI part different from a generic AI course?

It is not a tour of AI tools. Four of the ten sessions run AI against strategy work you have already done by hand in the first six: AI-powered PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, TOWS and Value Chain analysis; AI scenario planning and opportunity mapping; AI-driven OKRs, KPIs, Balanced Scorecards, roadmaps and dashboards. The judgement comes first, the acceleration second.

How do I know it fits my situation?

It fits if you are carrying an unclear strategy, weak profitability, missing systems or slow decision-making, and you want a structured way through rather than another framework list. To test that against your specific case first, use Apply Now or message +20 109 271 8547 and describe the challenge before you commit.
